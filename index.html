I am a third year PhD student at the Université du Littoral Côte d'Opale under the supervision of Nicolas Chenavier, David Coupier, and Arnaud Rousselle.

I am working in probability theory and my thesis work is about IDLA aggregates and trees (some simulations are given below).

Python GUI 🐍

I have developed an easy-to-use GUI in Python to simulate some IDLA aggregates. It is available on my GitHub, by clicking here. You will be able to simulate IDLA aggregates and trees in 2D and 3D, along with some multi-source aggregates, which is what my research focuses on. Each user has the possibility to save the generated simulations. More info is available on the GitHub page.
